                                                      - ## Grading Criteria for Diagrams
                                                     
                                                      - ### Basic Diagram (4 marks)
                                                      - - Correct axes labels (1)
                                                        - - Correct curves (1)
                                                          - - Equilibrium labeled (1)
                                                            - - All components labeled (1)
                                                             
                                                              - ### Shift Diagram (4 marks)
                                                              - - Original diagram correct (1)
                                                                - - Correct shift direction (1)
                                                                  - - New equilibrium marked (1)
                                                                    - - Arrows and labels (1)
                                                                     
                                                                      - ### Advanced Diagram (6 marks)
                                                                      - - Correct structure (2)
                                                                        - - Key features identified (2)
                                                                          - - Welfare loss/areas shown (1)
                                                                            - - Full labeling (1)
                                                                             
                                                                              - ## Common Examiner Tips
                                                                              - 1. Always label both axes clearly
                                                                                2. 2. Label all curves at the END of the line
                                                                                   3. 3. Use arrows to show direction of shift
                                                                                      4. 4. Mark original (E) and new equilibrium (E1) clearly
                                                                                         5. 5. Shade relevant areas (welfare loss, profit)
                                                                                            6. 6. Reference the diagram in your written answer
