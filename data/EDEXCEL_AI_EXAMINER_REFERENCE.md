# Edexcel IAL Economics AI Examiner Reference Guide

## Overview
This document provides comprehensive guidance for an AI examiner grading Edexcel International A Level Economics responses. The examiner should be **slightly strict** to promote better learning while remaining fair.

---

## Assessment Objectives (AO)

### AO1: Knowledge and Understanding (20-25%)
**What it tests:** Demonstration of knowledge and understanding of economic concepts, theories and models

**How to award marks:**
- Full marks: Precise definitions using correct economic terminology
- Partial marks: Basic understanding shown but lacking precision
- No marks: Incorrect, irrelevant or absent definitions

### AO2: Application (20-25%)
**What it tests:** Application of knowledge to economic contexts

**How to award marks:**
- Full marks: Clear, specific application to the given context/extract
- Partial marks: Some reference to context but lacking depth
- No marks: Generic answer with no contextual application

### AO3: Analysis (25-30%)
**What it tests:** Analysis of economic issues using appropriate methods

**How to award marks:**
- Full marks: Developed chains of reasoning with clear cause-effect relationships
- Partial marks: Some analysis but incomplete chains of reasoning
- No marks: Purely descriptive, no analytical reasoning

### AO4: Evaluation (25-30%)
**What it tests:** Evaluation of economic arguments and evidence

**How to award marks:**
- Full marks: Developed evaluation with chains of reasoning and informed judgement
- Partial marks: Evaluative points identified but not developed
- No marks: No attempt at evaluation or purely descriptive

## Command Word Guide

### Define (2 marks)
**AO Split:** AO1: 2

**Requirements:**
- Definition ONLY required
- No examples from context needed
- No application marks

**Mark Allocation:**
| Marks | Descriptor |
|-------|------------|
| 2 | Comprehensive, precise definition using correct terminology |
| 1 | Basic definition showing limited understanding |
| 0 | Incorrect or irrelevant |

---

### Draw (4 marks)
**AO Split:** AO1: 1, AO2: 3

**Mark Allocation:**
| Marks | Component |
|-------|-----------|
| 1 | Original supply and demand curves drawn |
| 1 | Correct shift drawn |
| 1 | New price labelled |
| 1 | New quantity labelled |

**Key Requirements:**
- Use directional arrows for shifts
- Label curves at END of line (D, S, D1, S1)
- Show original AND new equilibrium
- Written explanation NOT required if diagram is complete

---

### Calculate (4 marks)
**AO Split:** AO1: 1, AO2: 3

**Mark Allocation:**
| Marks | Component |
|-------|-----------|
| 1 | Correct formula stated |
| 3 | Correct calculations and final answer |

**Critical Points:**
- Formula MUST be shown first
- All working must be shown
- Units MUST be included in answer
- PED is ALWAYS negative (include minus sign)
- Elasticities are NOT percentages (no % sign)

---

### Explain (4 marks)

**Type 1:** (No analysis required)
- AO Split: AO1: 2, AO2: 2
- Triggers: "explain the difference", "explain what is meant by", "explain the term"

**Type 2:** (Analysis required)
- AO Split: AO1: 1, AO2: 1, AO3: 2
- Triggers: "explain one", "explain the likely impact", "illustrate with diagram"
- If diagram asked: 2 marks available for diagram

---

### Analyse (6 marks)
**AO Split:** AO1: 2, AO2: 2, AO3: 2

**IMPORTANT:** No evaluation marks available - do NOT reward evaluation

**With Diagram:**
- 1 K mark for original curves and equilibrium
- 1 Ap mark for correct shift
- 1 An mark for new equilibrium showing price/quantity changes

---

### Examine (8 marks)
**AO Split:** AO1: 2, AO2: 2, AO3: 2, AO4: 2

**Mark Allocation:**
| Component | Marks | Requirements |
|-----------|-------|--------------|
| Knowledge | 2 | Definition + identifying factor from case study |
| Application/Analysis | 4 | Up to 2 marks per linked explanation |
| Evaluation | 2 | 2 evaluative points OR 1 developed evaluation |

**Level Descriptors:**
| Level | Marks | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| 3 | 7-8 | Strong knowledge, developed analysis, supported evaluation in context |
| 2 | 4-6 | Sound knowledge, some analysis, limited evaluation |
| 1 | 1-3 | Basic knowledge, weak analysis, no evaluation |

**Examiner Notes:**
- Maximum 3 factors needed
- Evaluation IS REQUIRED
- Penalise overwriting (timing issue)

---

### Discuss (14 marks)
**AO Split:** KAA: 8 marks, Evaluation: 6 marks

**KAA Levels:**
| Level | Marks | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| 3 | 7-8 | Excellent knowledge, fully in context, developed chains, accurate diagrams |
| 2 | 4-6 | Sound knowledge, some context, basic analysis |
| 1 | 1-3 | Generic answer with limited/no context |

**Evaluation Levels:**
| Level | Marks | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| 3 | 5-6 | Developed chains of reasoning, in context, informed judgement |
| 2 | 3-4 | Some development of evaluative points |
| 1 | 1-2 | Generic/undeveloped evaluation |

**Key Requirements:**
- Context is CRITICAL (generic = Level 1 max)
- Diagram required if asked
- Use extract data as evaluation starting point
- Informed judgement needed for top marks

---

### Evaluate (20 marks)
**AO Split:** KAA: 12 marks, Evaluation: 8 marks

**KAA Levels:**
| Level | Marks | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| 3 | 9-12 | Precise concepts, developed chains, accurate diagrams, full context |
| 2 | 5-8 | Sound knowledge, some analysis, partial context |
| 1 | 1-4 | Basic knowledge, limited analysis, no context |

**Evaluation Levels:**
| Level | Marks | Descriptor |
|-------|-------|------------|
| 3 | 7-8 | Developed chains in context, informed judgement |
| 2 | 4-6 | Partially developed, some context |
| 1 | 1-3 | Undeveloped points |

**Essay Requirements:**
- Clear paragraph structure (NOT bullet points)
- 2-3 well-developed analysis points
- 3-4 evaluation points
- Informed judgement/conclusion
- Relevant diagrams explained

## Evaluation Framework: CLASPP

Use this framework to assess quality of evaluation:

| Letter | Meaning | Example |
|--------|---------|---------|
| **C** | Conclusion/Judgement | "On balance, the policy is likely to be effective because..." |
| **L** | Long run vs Short run | "In the short run X, but in the long run Y" |
| **A** | Assumptions/Limitations | "This assumes that... however in reality..." |
| **S** | Stakeholder impact | "While consumers benefit, producers may suffer because..." |
| **P** | Priorities | "The effectiveness depends on whether the priority is X or Y" |
| **P** | Pros vs Cons | "While this policy has benefits such as X, there are also drawbacks including Y" |

## Chains of Reasoning

**Definition:** A logical sequence of cause-effect statements demonstrating economic understanding

**Structure:** Factor A → Causes B → Which leads to C → Therefore outcome D

**Connective Phrases:**
- "This leads to..."
- "As a result..."
- "Consequently..."
- "Therefore..."
- "This means that..."
- "Which causes..."

**Example (Demand Increase):**
> Increase in consumer income → Consumers have more disposable income → Demand for normal goods increases → Demand curve shifts right from D to D1 → At original price, shortage exists → Price is bid up → Movement along supply curve → New equilibrium at higher price P1 and higher quantity Q1

## Common Mistakes to Penalise

### Diagrams
- Unlabelled axes
- Unlabelled curves
- Wrong direction of shift
- Missing equilibrium points
- Confusing shift vs movement along curve

### Calculations
- No formula shown
- Missing units
- Missing negative sign for PED
- Adding % to elasticities

### Essays
- No key term definitions
- Generic with no context
- Bullet points instead of paragraphs
- Undeveloped evaluation
- No informed judgement
- Separate points without chains

---

## Grading Tone

The AI examiner should be:

1. **Slightly Strict:** Hold students to high standards to promote learning
2. **Fair:** Award marks where deserved according to mark scheme
3. **Constructive:** Provide specific feedback for improvement
4. **Educational:** Explain why marks were/weren't awarded

**Feedback Template:**
```
Mark: X/Y

Strengths:
- [Specific strength 1]
- [Specific strength 2]

Areas for Improvement:
- [Specific improvement with guidance]
- [What was missing and how to include it]

To achieve higher marks:
- [Actionable advice]
```

## Key Terminology by Unit

### Unit 1: Markets in Action
scarcity, opportunity cost, PPF, demand, supply, equilibrium, elasticity, consumer surplus, producer surplus, market failure, externality, public good, merit good, demerit good

### Unit 2: Macroeconomic Performance
GDP, inflation, unemployment, balance of payments, aggregate demand, aggregate supply, multiplier, fiscal policy, monetary policy, supply-side policy

### Unit 3: Business Behaviour
costs, revenue, profit, economies of scale, perfect competition, monopoly, oligopoly, contestability, labour market, MRP, wage determination

### Unit 4: Global Economy
globalisation, comparative advantage, tariffs, exchange rates, development, poverty, inequality
